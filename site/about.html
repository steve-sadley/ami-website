About the Asbestos Management Institute

The professional body recognising and promoting best practice among the individuals who manage asbestos risk across the UK.

Who we are

AMI is the professional body for individuals working across the asbestos industry, from removal and analysis to surveying, consultancy and duty holder management. It exists to raise standards, recognise competence and support its members throughout their careers.

Membership recognises competence in people rather than in companies, and it is assessed on evidence. It is maintained by continuing professional development recorded each year against a published requirement, and it is open to independent verification by anybody who needs to rely on it.

What we stand for

Our purpose

Recognise

Grades and post nominals that give members credible, independent recognition of their competence.

Promote

Clear standards and guidance that raise the quality and safety of asbestos work across the UK.

Support

A community, continuing development and practical help so members can grow and stay current throughout their careers.

What we do

Setting and sharing best practice

AMI brings the industry together to agree what good looks like, publishes practical guidance, and gives members the development and recognition they need to demonstrate it.

Through seminars, webinars and the Annual General Meeting, members stay current and connected across the whole sector.
