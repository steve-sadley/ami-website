Membership and joining AMI

Recognition that shows employers, clients and duty holders you meet the standard for best practice in asbestos management.

Why join the Asbestos Management Institute

AMI is the professional body for individuals across the asbestos industry, from removal and analysis to surveying, consultancy and duty holder management. Membership is a mark of competence and a commitment to continuing professional development.

Whichever grade you join at, you become part of a recognised community with access to guidance, events and support, and you can use your membership to demonstrate your professional standing. Every current member appears on a public register that anybody can check.

Questions

Membership questions

Who can join AMI?

Membership is open to individuals working in or supporting the asbestos industry, including removal, analysis, surveying, consultancy and duty holder management. AMI recognises individuals rather than companies.

How much does membership cost?

Fees vary by grade and are shown against each grade above. Membership is invoiced by the office once an application has been approved. The site does not take payment.

Can I move up a grade later?

Yes. Membership grows with your career, and you can apply to move up a grade as your experience develops and your evidence supports it.

What is the CPD requirement?

Members record continuing professional development each year against a published requirement. Points are earned by attending AMI events, completing assessments and logging your own development, and every entry is reviewed by the office.

Does my membership appear publicly?

Every current member appears on the public register, so a client or duty holder can check a membership without having to ask you for proof. Your photograph appears on your profile only if you switch it on.

What happens if I let it lapse?

A membership that is not current is removed from the public register. The office handles renewals by hand and will contact you before that happens.
