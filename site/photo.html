What happens

After you press save

  1. Your browser cuts the two pictures and sends only those. The original never leaves your device, so the location and camera details buried in it never leave either.
  2. The upload goes through AMI rather than straight to storage, so the file is checked on arrival: the type, the real signature rather than the name, the size and the dimensions.
  3. Both land in the private photograph store. Nothing there can be read by an address alone, and every view is a signed link that expires.
  4. Your card picks up the new strip on its own. There is nothing to reinstall and nothing to reissue.
  5. It appears publicly only if the switch on your profile says so, and that switch is off until you turn it on.
What AMI asks for. Head and shoulders, looking at the camera, in reasonable light. At least 600 pixels on the short side. JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 20MB. No sunglasses and no hard hat pulled down over the eyes, because the point of the picture is that somebody at a gate can match it to the person in front of them.
